What Is Health Awareness?

Health awareness means understanding how to maintain physical, mental and social well-being. Students can improve their quality of life by following healthy habits and learning about disease prevention.

Importance of Personal Hygiene

🧼 Hand Hygiene

Wash your hands with soap and water, especially before eating and after using the toilet.

🪥 Oral Hygiene

Brush your teeth twice a day and maintain regular dental care.

🚿 Personal Cleanliness

Bathe regularly, wear clean clothes and keep your surroundings tidy.

🥗 Healthy Nutrition

Eat a balanced diet with fruits, vegetables, whole grains and protein.

Healthy Lifestyle Tips

  • Drink safe and clean water.
  • Eat nutritious and balanced meals.
  • Exercise regularly and stay active.
  • Get enough sleep every night.
  • Keep classrooms and surroundings clean.
  • Seek medical advice when necessary.
  • Take care of your mental well-being.

About This Project

This project aims to spread awareness about the importance of health and hygiene among students. It encourages simple daily practices that support a cleaner environment and a healthier lifestyle.
